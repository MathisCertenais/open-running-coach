# Coach Running — One-page proposal

**Your Strava, Garmin and OpenTracks data — turned into a coach that actually knows your training.**

## The problem

Runners are data-rich and insight-poor. Strava shows maps, Garmin shows widgets, OpenTracks records tracks — none tells you whether you're at injury risk, training polarized, or what to do for your next race. A human coach is expensive; generic AI chatbots don't have your data.

## The solution

A privacy-first running intelligence platform: import your raw files, get the metrics that matter, and an AI coach that explains them and builds your race plan.

## How it works

1. **Import** — KMZ (OpenTracks), GPX (Strava), TCX (Garmin). Duplicate runs across sources are merged automatically.
2. **Analyze** — injury risk (ACWR), fitness/fatigue/form (CTL/ATL/TSB), 80/20 intensity, VO2max and race predictions, splits/laps/GAP.
3. **Coach** — explains your numbers, answers questions, reviews your week, and builds a periodized plan to your race.

## The metrics

- **Injury risk** — acute vs chronic load (ACWR), with the 0.8–1.3 sweet spot.
- **Fitness / fatigue / form** — are you building or burning out?
- **80/20 intensity** — polarized, or stuck in the grey zone?
- **VO2max & race predictor** — realistic times from 400 m to marathon.
- **Session-RPE + post-run check-in** — sleep, stress, structured pain log, race flag.

Every metric is explainable: what it means, the formula, and how to act on it.

## The AI coach

Grounded in your data — it never invents numbers. It explains, answers, reviews and plans; flags red flags; never diagnoses injuries. Run it on a local model for full privacy, or **delegate it to us**.

## Privacy by design

Local-first. Your files and database stay on your machine. AI is opt-in; only derived metrics are sent — never GPS coordinates.

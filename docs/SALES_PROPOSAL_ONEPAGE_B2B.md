# Coach Running for Coaches & Clubs — One-page proposal (B2B)

**Monitor every athlete's load, injury risk and readiness — with an AI that does the first pass for you.**

## The problem

Athletes train on Strava, Garmin and OpenTracks; data is scattered. Coaches lose hours chasing files and miss early warning signs — load spikes, recurring pain, poor sleep — across 10–50 athletes. Most tools show charts, not priorities.

## The solution

A coach workspace on top of a proven analytics engine: one roster, risk alerts, per-athlete briefings, AI team summaries, plan assignment and reports.

## How it works

1. **Athletes keep their free app** (Strava / Garmin / OpenTracks).
2. **Data is normalized and analyzed** — ACWR, CTL/ATL/TSB, 80/20, VO2max, RPE, pain.
3. **You get a roster, alerts and AI briefings** — assign plans and export reports.

## What the coach sees

- **Roster** — load, ACWR band, form, readiness, pain, compliance.
- **Alerts** — high-risk ACWR, >30% load jump, recurring pain, red flags.
- **AI briefing** — "who's at risk, who's ready, who's off-plan."
- **Reports** — weekly PDF/CSV per athlete or squad.

## Phased and honest

- **Phase 1** — coach report export (fast validation).
- **Phase 2** — read-only coach workspace pilot.
- **Phase 3** — full multi-athlete platform (accounts, sync, billing).

## Privacy

Athlete opt-in. Derived metrics only for AI. Local-first option. GDPR-minded.

## Why Coach Running

Analytics already built · device-agnostic · explainable metrics + AI · privacy-first — numbers coaches can defend to athletes.

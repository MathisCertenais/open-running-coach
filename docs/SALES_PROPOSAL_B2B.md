# Coach Running for Coaches & Clubs — Sales Proposal (B2B)

**Monitor every athlete's load, injury risk and readiness — with an AI that does the first pass for you.**

---

## Executive summary

Coach Running is a running intelligence platform for coaches and clubs. It ingests athletes' existing activity data (Strava, Garmin, OpenTracks), computes the metrics that matter — **injury risk (ACWR), fitness/fatigue/form, 80/20 intensity, VO2max and race predictions** — and gives coaches a single workspace to monitor a roster, catch risk early and plan training.

The analytics engine already exists and is proven on real data. The B2B layer adds aggregation, alerts, reporting and collaboration on top — so you get a credible coaching product without starting from zero.

## The problem for coaches

- Athletes train on **different devices**; data is scattered across Strava, Garmin and OpenTracks.
- Coaches lose hours every week **chasing files and rebuilding spreadsheets**.
- **Early warning signs** — load spikes, recurring pain, poor sleep, high RPE on easy runs — are easy to miss across 10–50 athletes.
- Most tools show **charts, not priorities**. Coaches need "who needs attention this week?", not another dashboard.
- **Privacy and consent**: athletes and clubs increasingly want control over health data.

## The solution

A coach workspace built on the same analytics engine as the free athlete app:

- **Roster view** — one line per athlete: load, ACWR band, form, readiness, pain flag, plan compliance.
- **Risk alerts** — athletes entering the high-risk ACWR band, a >30% week-on-week jump, recurring pain, or red flags.
- **Per-athlete briefing** — the same metrics the athlete sees, plus trends and adherence.
- **Team / athlete AI summaries** — a morning brief: "who's at risk, who's ready, who's off-plan."
- **Plan assignment** — assign a periodized plan (or let the AI draft one) and track completion.
- **Reports** — a clean weekly PDF/CSV per athlete or for the whole squad.

## The AI advantage for coaches

- **Triage first** — "3 athletes in the high-risk band, 2 reporting recurring pain, 1 off-plan."
- **Per-athlete briefing** — a five-line summary the coach can act on immediately.
- **Plan drafting** — a first-draft periodized plan per athlete, editable by the coach.
- **Guardrails** — uses only the data, flags uncertainty, never diagnoses, and escalates medical concerns.

## How athletes' data gets in (phased — honest)

**Phase 1 — Coach report (available first).**
Athletes use the free app and export a weekly report (PDF/CSV). Coaches get structured, comparable data without changing anyone's workflow. *Lowest effort, fastest validation.*

**Phase 2 — Coach workspace (pilot).**
Athletes opt in and share their data; the coach sees a read-only roster with alerts and AI summaries. No complex accounts yet.

**Phase 3 — Full platform.**
Accounts and roles, sync/upload, plan assignment, billing and club-level administration.

## Privacy & consent

- **Athletes own their data**; sharing with a coach is **opt-in**.
- **Data minimisation**: derived metrics (not raw GPS) are used for AI summaries.
- **Local-first option** for privacy-sensitive athletes and clubs.
- **GDPR-minded**: consent, access and deletion.

## Why Coach Running

- **Analytics engine already built** — faster to a credible B2B product than starting from zero.
- **Device-agnostic** — Strava, Garmin, OpenTracks.
- **Explainable metrics + AI** — coaches trust numbers they can defend to athletes.
- **Privacy-first** — a real differentiator with clubs and athletes.
